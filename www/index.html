How to play

Moving

  • Slide your left thumb on the round pad, or use the arrow keys: left and right to walk, up to jump, down to duck.
  • Walking away from the enemy also guards, so you block their attacks automatically.

Attacking

  • PUNCH is fast. KICK is stronger and reaches further.
  • Duck and press KICK for a low sweep that knocks the enemy down.
  • Press PUNCH or KICK in the air for a jumping attack.
  • Hold BLOCK to take almost no damage. Duck while blocking to stop low sweeps.

One-tap specials

Every fighter has SHOT, RISE, RUSH and SUPER buttons, but each one works differently depending on the fighting style.

Beast mode

When your gold bar is full, SUPER lights up. Press it and your fighter powers up in a cinematic, transforms into a beast form, and unleashes their super move. The beast form lasts about 7 seconds and gives 20% more power and speed. Every fighter has a different transformation.

Fighting styles

  • BLAZE, Fire Kickboxing. Balanced. Fireball, flaming uppercut, 2-hit rush. Transforms into Inferno Form and throws a giant 5-hit fireball.
  • FROST, Ice Ninjutsu. Double jump (press up again in the air), dive kick (KICK in the air), shards in the air, a spin that passes through shots. Transforms into Glacier Wraith and freezes the enemy solid.
  • TITAN, Mountain Wrestling. Armored attacks keep going through hits. RISE is an unblockable grab. Transforms into Stone Colossus and sends an earthquake along the ground.
  • VOLT, Lightning Speed. 2-hit jab, low slide kick, lightning from the sky, teleport strike. Transforms into Storm Avatar and rains six lightning bolts.
  • JADE, Wind Tai Chi. Wind waves push enemies far back. RUSH blasts them into the wall so they bounce back for a combo. Transforms into Wind Spirit, floats, and sends a tornado.
  • NOX, Shadow Counter Arts. RUSH is a counter stance: if they hit you during it, Nox vanishes behind them and strikes back. SHOT hides a snare in the ground. RISE steals health. Transforms into Shadow Demon with wings and horns.
  • MARINA, Water Capoeira. Swaying stance and handstand kicks. RUSH is a cartwheel that hits from above, so duck-blocking fails. SHOT makes a geyser burst from the ground. Transforms into Leviathan and summons a tidal wave.
  • RIVET, Cyborg Boxing. Rocket Fist flies out and comes back, hitting twice. Jet Uppercut, and a 6-punch piston barrage. Transforms into Overdrive Mech with jet thrusters and fires a full-screen laser.
  • SOLA, Sun Kung Fu. Halo Disc travels in a wave. RISE is a halo that reflects enemy shots back. RUSH launches the enemy into the air for a juggle. Transforms into Sun Avatar with a halo and wings of light, then explodes in a solar flare.
  • HEX, Venom Drunken Fist. Wobbly drunken stance. Poison clouds, a low weaving dash, and bites that poison the enemy so they lose health over time. Transforms into Serpent Beast with a cobra hood and tail.

Easy combos

  • PUNCH, PUNCH, RUSH
  • PUNCH, KICK, RISE
  • Jump-in KICK, PUNCH, SUPER

Tap the next button right after a hit lands and the moves chain together.
